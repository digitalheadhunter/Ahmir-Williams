How it works

From family recipe to retail shelf, in three steps

We built ShelfReady so you don't need a food science degree or a warehouse to get your recipe in front of real customers.

1

Submit your recipe

Tell us about your recipe, your current batch size, and what you're picturing on the shelf. Takes a few minutes, no commitment.

2

We handle formulation, packaging & compliance

Our team helps you scale the recipe reliably, choose the right packaging, and points you toward the right compliance and labeling resources.

3

Launch to retail

Get a shelf-ready, pitch-ready product to bring to local shops, specialty grocers, farmers markets, and beyond.
